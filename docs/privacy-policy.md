# Privacy Policy for MultiCalc

MultiCalc is made by Step Into The Code. This policy explains what the app does with
your information. It is short because the app does very little with it.

## The short version

**MultiCalc collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to.** There is no account,
no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## What the app stores

Everything you do in MultiCalc is written to the app's own private storage on your
phone, where no other app can read it:

- the calculators you have open, their names and their colours
- what is currently on each display
- each calculator's history of past calculations
- your theme and vibration settings

Android keeps this area private to the app. It is removed when you uninstall MultiCalc,
and you can clear it at any time from your phone's Settings, under Apps, MultiCalc,
Storage.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to send it.

- **Export history.** When you tap Export, the app hands a file to Android's own save
  dialog and you choose where it goes. Where it lands after that is up to you and the
  app has no further part in it.
- **Support link.** Tapping "Support this project" opens a page in your browser. That
  page is served by GitHub and is subject to
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  MultiCalc sends nothing with the link about you or your use of the app.

## Permissions

MultiCalc asks for one permission, `VIBRATE`, so keys can give a short buzz. You can
turn that off in the app's Settings.

The app also declares, in its manifest, that it needs to see which app on your phone is
a calculator. This is how Android's "package visibility" works: without the declaration
the app cannot find your calculator to open a second copy of it. It is a declaration
about what the app can look up, not a permission you grant, and nothing about the apps
on your phone is recorded or transmitted.

MultiCalc does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone,
files, or any other permission.

## Children

MultiCalc is a calculator. It contains no advertising, no purchases, no user generated
content, no social features and no data collection, so there is nothing in it that
treats children differently from anyone else.
